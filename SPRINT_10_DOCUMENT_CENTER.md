# Filexa Sprint 10 — PDF & Document Center

## Added
- New Document Center reachable from Home → PDF center.
- Automatic grouping for PDF, Word, spreadsheet, presentation, and text files.
- Search, document-type filters, sorting, pull-to-refresh, and list/grid layouts.
- File actions: open, share, favorite for the current session, and details.
- Internal text reader for TXT, Markdown, JSON, and CSV files up to 2 MB.
- Text reader controls for font size, line wrapping, selectable text, and sharing.
- PDF and Office files open through the best compatible viewer installed on Android.
- Responsive empty, loading, and error states using the Filexa design system.

## Version
- 1.10.0+22

## Notes
- Advanced PDF editing tools such as merge, split, compression, and annotation are not included in this sprint.
- Favorites in Document Center are session-based in this build and will be persisted in a later storage sprint.
